No new critical-resolution claim is added.

**Confirmed** denotes demonstrated source flow, not necessarily device exploitation. **Risk** is an architectural limitation/precondition; **untested** requires acceptance evidence. Original severity is retained, not rescored as hypothetical privilege escalation. No CRITICAL finding was substantiated in the independent audits; that is not proof of absence.

**FIXED** applies only to the specific defect and named executed scope; **MITIGATED** retains stated limitations; **UNVERIFIED** means missing execution/acceptance for that boundary, not necessarily uncompiled source; **DEFERRED** is outstanding work; **NOT APPLICABLE** is a hypothesis absent under pinned defaults. Host fixes do not imply whole-app/device approval.
